# ASSET-MANIFEST.md — Ahi o te Manawa Website

This document is the complete inventory of every image asset used on the site, with its location, purpose, suggested local filename, and treatment. It is self-contained and does not require viewing any image or PDF file.

Note: real photo/image files have not yet been supplied separately from the original design mockups. The filenames below are the **target filenames to use once real assets are provided**. Until then, use plain grey placeholder boxes at the correct aspect ratio in their place, sized per the "Treatment" column, so the layout can be built and tested before final photos arrive.

There are **22 image assets** across the site, all on the Home and Events pages. The Purpose page and Contact page (shared-header shell only, no content yet) contain no images beyond the shared header logo and footer.
