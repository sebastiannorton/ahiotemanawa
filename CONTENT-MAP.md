# CONTENT-MAP.md — Ahi o te Manawa Website

This document is the single source of truth for **exact text content**, page/section order, and heading hierarchy across the site. It reflects the following confirmed client decisions:

- The page previously called "About" is renamed to **"Purpose"** (nav label: `purpose`).
- The **Home** page keeps its original structure and content as previously documented — it is not being restructured or merged with the Purpose page.
- The **Gallery** page and nav item are **removed entirely**.
- Spelling typos, missing apostrophes, inconsistent acronym capitalisation, and one factual name typo have been corrected (full list in Section 6 below).
- A basic **footer** has been added to every page (content and styling below; also see DESIGN-ANALYSIS.md Section 7).

Original stylised lowercase text (e.g. nav items, section headings like "location", "the spaces", the phrase "ahi o te manawa" used inside running text/headings) is a **deliberate brand style**, not a typo, and has been preserved as-is. Only genuine spelling/grammar errors were corrected — see Section 6 for the exact list of what changed and why.
